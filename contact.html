Frequently Asked Questions

Do you offer vegan or non-dairy milk options? +

Yes! We offer Oat milk, Almond milk, and Soy milk for any of our espresso beverages.

Can I buy coffee beans for home brewing? +

Absolutely. All of our signature roasted beans are available for purchase in 250g and 1kg bags in-store or online.

Is WiFi available for guests? +

Yes, high-speed complimentary WiFi is provided for all patrons in our coffee bar.
