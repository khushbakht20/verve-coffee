Our Story & Passion

Crafting exceptional coffee experiences since 2010

Established 2010

A Decade of Specialty Coffee

Founded in 2010, Verve Coffee started as a small neighborhood coffee bar with a single mission: to roast and brew coffee that respects both the farm standard and the customer palate.

Over the years, we have grown into an artisan roasting powerhouse while maintaining our commitment to small-batch crafting, direct trade farmer partnerships, and welcoming community spaces.

Our Core Values

Guided by principles of quality, transparency, and craft

1. Direct Trade

We work directly with smallholder coffee farms, paying premium prices above fair-trade standards to support sustainable farming ecosystems.

2. Precision Roasting

Our head roasters tailor roast profiles for every bean origin to accentuate bright natural notes, fruit sweetness, and floral aromas.

3. Warm Hospitality

Every cup is served with genuine warmth. We believe great coffee is best enjoyed in an inviting, inspiring setting.
